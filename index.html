About A4H&S

A4H&S — Art for Heart & Soul — is the creative identity of artist Sonal, whose work spans oil, acrylic, and mixed media. Each piece is painted with devotion, born from a deep dialogue between emotion and pigment.

From intimate canvases to monumental murals, from devotional subjects rooted in India’s spiritual heritage to bold explorations of feminine power and the natural world, every work invites the viewer into a direct, wordless encounter with feeling.

All works are one-of-a-kind originals, available for private collection worldwide.
